# Retail Business Performance & KPI Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **Retail Business Performance & KPI Analysis** designed to evaluate the operational, financial, customer, and strategic performance of a multi-city omnichannel retail business.

The project uses a **synthetic transaction-level dataset** to demonstrate how business data can be transformed into actionable management insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The analysis focuses on:

- Revenue and sales performance
- Gross profit and gross margin analysis
- Store-level productivity
- Product category performance
- Customer economics
- Customer segmentation
- Omnichannel performance
- KPI development
- SWOT analysis
- Business risk analysis
- Financial recommendations
- Strategic growth planning
- Implementation roadmap
